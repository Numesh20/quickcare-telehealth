Lead Time vs. Cycle Time

Lead Time measures total duration from story creation to user hands (4.2 days). Cycle Time measures only active work duration once work begins (1.8 days).

Little's Law Formula
Cycle Time = WIP / Throughput

Mathematically proves that reducing Work In Progress (WIP) directly shortens Cycle Time without adding headcount.

CFD Horizontal & Vertical Bands

Horizontal distance between top and bottom bands represents Cycle Time. Vertical distance at any point shows total Work In Progress (WIP).

Flow Efficiency Metric
Flow Eff. = (Touch Time / Lead Time) × 100
